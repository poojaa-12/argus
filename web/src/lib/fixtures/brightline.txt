CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION MEMORANDUM

Brightline Commerce, Inc.

Brightline is a B2B software / vertical SaaS company based in Boston, MA.

The platform sells workflow software to mid-market distributors. FY2025 total revenue was $62.5 million, up 24% year-over-year. Adjusted EBITDA was $14.1 million (23% margin). Net debt / EBITDA stands at 3.1x. Recurring revenue mix is 88%. The company has 240 employees.

Key risks
- ERP switching costs at two lighthouse accounts
- Implementation cycle can slip a quarter
